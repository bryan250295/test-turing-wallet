抵押 TBC，借出 TuringUSD

想保留手中的 TBC，又需要一笔链上资金？你可以把 TBC 锁入智能合约，借出 TuringUSD。还清债务后，就可以按协议取回仓位中剩余的 TBC。

TuringUSD 是 TBC 链上的稳定币，价格目标为 1 美元。借款时，协议根据你的抵押仓位发行 TuringUSD，无需等待另一位用户出借。TuringUSD 的市场价格可能偏离 1 美元，你的债务仍以 TuringUSD 计量。

同样多的 TBC，能借多少 TuringUSD？

按新版原生 TBC 的借款规则，抵押价值 2,000 美元的 TBC，最多可借 1,000 TuringUSD。借满时，抵押率正好是 200%（2,000 ÷ 1,000）。抵押估值采用协议认可的报价，实际额度还受仓位和系统上限约束。

首版网页使用原生 TBC 抵押。协议也支持经准入的 TBC20 Standard 代币。每个仓位只用一种抵押资产，各类资产可有不同的抵押率和利率，借出的都是 TuringUSD。

从抵押借款到还款

预览借款

在「总览」查看原生 TBC 的参考价格、最低抵押率和计息年率。填入要抵押的 TBC 与想借的 TuringUSD 后，页面会获取新报价，显示这笔借款的预计抵押率。

签名并确认

核对网络、金额和手续费，用自己的签名器先签准备交易，再签借款交易。私钥由你保管。借款交易确认后，到「我的仓位」查看抵押的 TBC 和欠下的 TuringUSD。

偿还并取回抵押品

在对应仓位选择「偿还 TuringUSD」，可部分还款或按当前债务还清。请通过还款入口操作，普通钱包转账不会抵减仓位债务。首版网页支持还款，清偿后提取剩余 TBC 可通过协议开发工具完成。

除了归还本金，还要支付哪些费用？

借款利息用 TuringUSD 计

利息随时间计入债务。页面年率按当前计息参数换算，参数可调整。还清仓位时，以当时的应还 TuringUSD 数量为准。

网络手续费用 TBC 付

钱包要为准备交易和借款交易分别留出 TBC。预览页会列出两笔费用。准备交易上链后，即使取消后续借款，已付的准备手续费也不会退回。

仓位管理

借了 1,000 TuringUSD，何时会清算？

接着看这笔借满的仓位。假设暂不计息，债务维持在 1,000 TuringUSD，按目标价折合 1,000 美元。原生 TBC 的清算线为 120%，对应这笔仓位的抵押总值为 1,200 美元。

同一笔 TBC，从抵押价值变化与清算线

抵押的 TBC 总值抵押率风险状态
2,000 美元200%恰好达到初始抵押率，已借满额度
1,800 美元180%低于初始抵押率，仍高于清算线
1,200 美元120%总值下跌 40%，恰好到线，此时还不能启动清算
1,199 美元119.9%跌破清算线，可以发起整仓拍卖

利息也计入债务。如果应还金额已增至 1,010 TuringUSD，清算线就升至 1,212 美元（1,010 × 120%）。

在清算前，怎样给仓位多留一些余地？

抵押总值为 1,800 美元时，偿还 100 TuringUSD，债务降至 900，抵押率就回到 200%（1,800 ÷ 900）。按同一报价，用协议开发工具追加价值 200 美元的 TBC，也能让抵押率回到 200%。

跌破 1,200 美元后，抵押的 TBC 怎么卖？

启动拍卖

按表中 1,199 美元这一行，任何人都可以提交清算交易，无需你签名。合约核验报价、债务和可用拍卖额度后，整个仓位进入拍卖。

买家分批买入

每枚 TBC 的起拍价为协议报价的 1.1 倍。例如，报价为 0.10 美元时，从 0.11 TuringUSD 起拍，随后按时间线性降价。买家选定数量，用 TuringUSD 按当时的拍卖价买走 TBC。若拍卖价降至本轮起拍价的 30%，可用新报价重开。

结算并退回余量

成交款先还 1,000 TuringUSD 债务，再支付 13% 的清算罚金（130 TuringUSD）。累计收足 1,130 TuringUSD 就结束拍卖，剩余 TBC 直接退回你的地址。若全部 TBC 卖完只收回 950 TuringUSD，协议将剩余 50 TuringUSD 记为坏账，并取消未收取的罚金。

Collateralize TBC, Borrow TuringUSD

Want to keep your TBC while accessing funds on-chain? Lock TBC in a smart contract to borrow TuringUSD. After repaying the debt, you can withdraw the TBC remaining in your position in accordance with the protocol.

TuringUSD is a stablecoin on the TBC blockchain that targets US$1. When you borrow, the protocol issues TuringUSD against your collateral position, so you do not need to wait for another user to lend it. TuringUSD may trade above or below US$1, but your debt remains denominated in TuringUSD.

How Much TuringUSD Can You Borrow Against the Same Amount of TBC?

Under the updated borrowing rules for native TBC, US$2,000 worth of TBC collateral supports a maximum loan of 1,000 TuringUSD. At that borrowing amount, the collateralization ratio is exactly 200% (2,000 ÷ 1,000). Collateral is valued using prices accepted by the protocol. Actual borrowing capacity is also subject to position-level and system-wide limits.

The initial web interface accepts native TBC as collateral. The protocol also supports approved TBC20 Standard tokens. Each position can use only one collateral asset. Different assets may have different collateralization requirements and interest rates, but all loans are issued in TuringUSD.

From Borrowing to Repayment

Preview Your Loan

On “Overview,” check the reference price of native TBC, the minimum collateralization ratio, and the annualized interest rate. Enter the amount of TBC you want to use as collateral and the amount of TuringUSD you want to borrow. The page will fetch a fresh quote and display the estimated collateralization ratio.

Sign and Confirm

Check the network, amounts, and fees. Use your own signing tool to sign the preparation transaction first, then sign the borrowing transaction. You remain in control of your private key. Once the borrowing transaction is confirmed, go to “My Positions” to view your TBC collateral and outstanding TuringUSD debt.

Repay and Recover Collateral

In the relevant position, select “Repay TuringUSD” to make a partial repayment or pay the current debt in full. Use the designated repayment function: an ordinary wallet transfer will not reduce the debt recorded for your position. The initial web interface supports repayment. After the debt is fully repaid, the remaining TBC can be withdrawn using the protocol developer tools.

What Fees Do You Pay in Addition to Principal?

Borrowing Interest Is Charged in TuringUSD

Interest accrues to your debt over time. The annualized rate shown on the page is calculated from the current interest parameters, which may be adjusted. When repaying in full, the amount due is the TuringUSD debt outstanding at that time.

Network Fees Are Paid in TBC

Keep enough TBC in your wallet to pay for the preparation transaction and the borrowing transaction separately. The preview page lists both fees. Once the preparation transaction is recorded on-chain, its fee is non-refundable, even if you cancel the subsequent borrowing transaction.

This guide describes the updated TBC parameters. The new rules take effect only after a new contract instance is deployed. Existing public testnet instances will continue to operate under their original contracts.

After Borrowing 1,000 TuringUSD, When Could Liquidation Occur?

Now consider this fully borrowed position. Assume no interest has accrued and the debt remains 1,000 TuringUSD, equivalent to US$1,000 at the target price. The liquidation threshold for native TBC is 120%, which means the total collateral value at the threshold is US$1,200.

How the Same TBC Collateral Moves Toward the Liquidation Threshold

Total Value of TBC CollateralCollateralization RatioRisk Status
US$2,000200%At the borrowing limit; the full available amount has been borrowed
US$1,800180%Below the initial borrowing ratio, but still above the liquidation threshold
US$1,200120%Collateral value has fallen 40% and reached the threshold; liquidation cannot be initiated at this exact level
US$1,199119.9%Below the liquidation threshold; a full-position auction can be initiated

Accrued interest is also included in the debt. If the amount owed increases to 1,010 TuringUSD, the liquidation threshold rises to US$1,212 (1,010 × 120%).

How Can You Create More Safety Margin Before Liquidation?

When total collateral is worth US$1,800, repaying 100 TuringUSD reduces the debt to 900 TuringUSD and raises the collateralization ratio to 200% (1,800 ÷ 900). At the same quoted price, you can also use the protocol developer tools to add US$200 worth of TBC, which likewise brings the collateralization ratio back to 200%.

How Is TBC Collateral Sold After Its Value Falls Below US$1,200?

Start the Auction

As shown in the US$1,199 row, anyone can submit a liquidation transaction without your signature. After the contract verifies the quote, debt, and available auction capacity, the entire position enters auction.

Buyers Purchase Collateral in Batches

The starting auction price for each TBC is 1.1 times the protocol quote. For example, if the quoted price is US$0.10, the auction starts at 0.11 TuringUSD per TBC and then decreases linearly over time. Buyers choose an amount and purchase TBC using TuringUSD at the current auction price. If the auction price falls to 30% of that round’s starting price, the auction can be restarted using a fresh quote.

Settle the Debt and Return Any Surplus

Auction proceeds first repay the 1,000 TuringUSD debt, then cover the 13% liquidation penalty (130 TuringUSD). Once a total of 1,130 TuringUSD has been collected, the auction ends and any remaining TBC is returned directly to your address. If all TBC is sold but only 950 TuringUSD is recovered, the protocol records the remaining 50 TuringUSD as bad debt and waives any unpaid penalty.
